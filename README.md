## Analytical lifecycle

```text
Customer behavior
       ↓
KPI / cohort measurement
       ↓
Segmentation and features
       ↓
Propensity / churn / timing models
       ↓
Experiment or campaign decision
       ↓
Revenue and retention impact
```
